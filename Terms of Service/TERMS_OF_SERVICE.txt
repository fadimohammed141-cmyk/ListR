1. Service

ListR provides commands for creating, viewing, and modifying personal anime lists and tracking an overall watched-episode total. Its documented commands are /addanime, /animestats, and /animereset.

ListR is an independent third-party Discord application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

2. Commands

/addanime adds an anime using the required title, category, and episodes options. The available categories are completed, watching, and interested. For interested, the episodes value should be 0.

The episodes option is not an episode count for the anime named in title. It is a separate global counter used only to track the user's total number of episodes watched overall.

/animestats displays the user's anime lists and overall recorded watched-episode total.

/animereset modifies existing ListR data. Its reset-wat option determines whether all data, a category, a specific anime, or the global watched-episode total is affected.

When reset-wat is episodes, the episode option changes the global watched-episode total and is not tied to an anime. A negative episode value adds episodes rather than removing them.

3. Conditional Command Options

/animereset has one required option, reset-wat, and three additional options: anime, category, and episode.

The additional options are conditionally required:

- reset-wat=all requires no additional option.
- reset-wat=category requires category.
- reset-wat=anime requires anime.
- reset-wat=episodes requires episode.

The anime option is not required and is not used when reset-wat is episodes.
