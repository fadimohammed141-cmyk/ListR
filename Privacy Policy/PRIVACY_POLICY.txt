ListR Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data ListR's persistent bot variables store, why that data is used, and how users can request its deletion.

ListR is an independent third-party Discord application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

1. Data Stored by ListR

ListR's persistent variables store only these categories of data:

- User IDs, and only when a User ID is required to associate stored data with the correct user.
- Anime names.
- Episode numbers.
- Chosen options used by ListR's functionality, such as the selected anime category or /animereset reset-wat option.

These are the only categories of information intentionally stored in ListR's persistent variables.

ListR does not intentionally store the following in its persistent ListR variables:

- Discord server IDs.
- Usernames or display names.
- Avatars.
- Message content.
- Discord passwords.
- Discord authentication tokens.
- Email addresses.
- Payment information.
- IP addresses.
- Precise location information.
- Voice or video data.
- Unrelated personal information.

If information is not one of the data categories expressly listed in this policy, it is not intentionally stored in ListR's persistent variables.

2. Why Data Is Stored

Each stored data category has a direct functional purpose:

- User IDs are used only when necessary to associate an anime list with the correct Discord user.
- Anime names are used to identify entries in an anime list.
- Episode numbers are used to track the number of episodes recorded for an anime.
- Chosen options are used to record or process the selections required by ListR's commands, such as a category or reset operation.

ListR does not use this stored data for advertising, selling personal information, or creating unrelated user profiles.

3. Commands and Data

/addanime accepts a title, category, and episode number so that an anime can be added to a list.

/animestats reads the relevant stored anime-list information so that the user's lists can be displayed.

/animereset uses the selected reset-wat option and any associated anime, category, or episode value to modify the user's existing list data.

For /animereset with reset-wat set to episodes, a negative episode value adds episodes instead of removing them.

4. Discord and Third-Party Data Access

ListR operates through Discord. Discord may make information available to a third-party application according to the permissions, APIs, and access rules applicable to that application.

This Privacy Policy describes what ListR intentionally stores in its own persistent variables. It does not describe or replace Discord's own privacy practices.

Discord states that apps can have access to certain baseline information depending on how they are installed and what access they have, and that users should review an app developer's privacy policy to understand the app's data practices. See Discord's official privacy and app-data documentation for details.

5. Data Sharing

ListR does not sell, rent, or intentionally share its stored ListR data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

If infrastructure or service providers are required to operate ListR, any data processing by those providers is limited to what is necessary to provide the service and is subject to their applicable terms and policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

ListR retains stored anime-list data for as long as it is needed to provide the service, unless it is deleted through ListR's functionality or by an applicable data-deletion request.

Users may request deletion of their stored ListR data by contacting the ListR owner through the official support or contact method provided with the bot or its project repository.

A deletion request may require the user to provide their Discord User ID or other information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant stored data. That information will be used for the deletion request and not intentionally stored as part of the user's ListR data unless required for the request.

If data is deleted, copies may remain temporarily in technical backups or logs where applicable and may be removed according to the relevant retention cycle.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored ListR data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a security incident materially affects stored user data, the ListR owner will take reasonable steps required by applicable law and the circumstances of the incident.

8. Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for the anime names, categories, and episode numbers they submit. ListR does not independently verify whether an anime title or episode count is accurate.

9. Children's Privacy

Users must meet Discord's applicable minimum age requirements and comply with Discord's policies. ListR does not intentionally request additional personal information from users beyond the limited data described in this policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ListR's commands, storage implementation, data practices, or legal requirements change.

If the categories of persistent data intentionally stored by ListR change, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those changes.

The current version published with ListR replaces previous versions.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the ListR owner through the official support or contact method provided with the bot or its project repository.

This document is a general privacy-policy template and is not legal advice. The ListR owner is responsible for ensuring that this policy accurately reflects the actual implementation, hosting/logging configuration, retention periods, and applicable laws.
